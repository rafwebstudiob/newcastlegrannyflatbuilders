Building a granny flat in Elermore Vale

Elermore Vale is a quiet western family suburb with mostly brick homes built from the 1970s onwards.

Sloping blocks need more site work, such as retaining, a raised floor or a split design, and careful drainage.

Post-war and 1980s homes often sit towards the front of the block, leaving a backyard that may suit a detached granny flat.
